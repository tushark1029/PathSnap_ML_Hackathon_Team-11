### Person 1 – Dataset Understanding

My responsibility was to understand the structure and organisation of the raw PathSnap dataset.

The dataset contains four types of files: `.track`, `.nodes`, `.arcs` and `.route`.

The `.track` files contain GPS observations with longitude, latitude and timestamp information. The `.nodes` files contain geographical coordinates of road-network nodes. The `.arcs` files represent connections between nodes, and `.route` files contain the reference sequence of road-segment IDs.

After inspecting the uploaded dataset, I identified 80 training trips and 10 student-testing trips, giving us 90 available trips.

The training partition contains 208,417 GPS observations, while the student-testing partition contains 19,160 observations.

I also calculated the number of nodes, arcs and reference-route entries for both partitions and prepared a detailed dataset inventory.

One important observation is that the number of GPS observations varies between trips. Therefore, our subsequent preprocessing pipeline must support variable-length trajectories.

I also identified that the evaluator-testing partition specified in the PRD is not included in the currently supplied dataset.

Overall, this analysis provides the structural understanding required for the next stages of data cleaning, feature extraction and machine-learning development.
